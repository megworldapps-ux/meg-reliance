India's Leading VFX & Animation Institute

The Only Academy Backed By A Production Studio

Reliance Animation Academy, a division of Reliance Animation Studios and a part of Reliance Entertainment has travelled an amazing path since its inception in 2009.

Reliance Animation Academy is a leading institute in India offering career-focused programs in Animation, VFX, Gaming, Graphics, and Film Making.

Its curriculum integrates the latest technologies, including AI and real-time production workflows, ensuring students are aligned with current industry demands.The academy continues to play a key role in shaping talent for the rapidly growing AVGC sector.

Vision

To create a world-class academy of Animation and VFX education, dedicated towards crafting leaders in moving arts on a global scale.

Mission

To provide high quality education that delivers a transformative learning experience with a holistic approach.To constantly innovate and improve our standards and technologies.

Reliance Animation Studios

Animation

Reliance Animation is an original IP production company established in 2005 with a vision to create fresh content and impeccable execution, bringing the best of both worlds together.

Reliance Animation is an award-winning studio in partnership with noted filmmaker ROHIT SHETTY, best known for delivering IP-driven animation content like Little Singham, Golmaal Jr., Little Krishna, and Smashing Simba.

Little Krishna was its first 3D production from India, written by Jeffrey Scott, followed by one of the greatest stories ever told – “Krishna aur Kans”, a theatrical spectacle released in August 2012. Reliance Animation produced an animated version of India's first superhero, 'Shaktimaan'.

The studio takes pride in producing 'The Davincibles' show, which was a co-production with Moonscoop US and Neo Network.

Reliance Entertainment

Entertainment

Reliance Entertainment, the only 100% Indian-owned corporate player in the filmed entertainment, is a place where commerce meets art, blockbusters meet meaningful cinema, Bollywood & Hollywood meet Indian regional cinema and where genres like drama and comedy meet horror and romance.

Reliance Entertainment has an industry acknowledged Joint Venture business model, wherein creative filmmakers come on-board as JV partners and jointly share the IPR ownership and its rewards.

With 300+ commercially and critically acclaimed movies in its portfolio, Reliance Entertainment has been distributing and syndicating films across India and 100+ countries.

Incorporated in 2016, Rohit Shetty Picturez LLP is a Reliance Entertainment joint venture with film director, producer and television personality Rohit Shetty.

The Academic Advisory Board: Shaping the Future of AVGC

At Reliance Animation Academy, we believe that education should never exist in a vacuum. To ensure our students are not just "job-ready" but "future-ready," we have established our Academic Advisory Board (AAB).

This elite council brings together the brightest technical minds and the most influential business leaders from the global AVGC ecosystem. By bridging the gap between the classroom and the studio, we ensure our curriculum evolves at the speed of imagination.

Objective

The primary mission of the AAB is to provide strategic counsel and industry oversight. The board works to:

✓

Align Curriculum with Industry Standards:

Regularly auditing our courses to ensure they reflect the latest pipelines, software, and workflows used in top-tier studios.

✓

Bridge the Talent Gap:

Identifying the specific skills currently in high demand within the technical and business sectors of the industry.

✓

Foster Innovation:

Encouraging the integration of emerging technologies like AI, Real-time Rendering, and Virtual Production into our academic framework.

Advantages for Our Students

The presence of the Academic Advisory Board offers our students a distinct competitive edge:

✓

Industry-Validated Learning:

Students graduate knowing their skills are exactly what the market is looking for.

✓

Direct Mentorship:

Opportunities for guest lectures, masterclasses, and "inside-the-studio" perspectives from board members.

✓

Enhanced Career Pathways:

Through the board's network, we facilitate stronger industry tie-ups, internships, and placement opportunities.

✓

Global Benchmarking:

Our standards are set not just locally, but against international creative benchmarks.
