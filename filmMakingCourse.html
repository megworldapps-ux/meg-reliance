The Film Making courses at Reliance Animation Academy introduce students to the creative and technical processes behind professional film and digital video production.

Students explore storytelling, script development, direction, cinematography, editing, production design, sound, and post-production through practical exercises and project-based learning.

The curriculum encourages students to work through the complete production workflow, from the first idea and planning stage to shooting, editing, finishing, and presentation.

Portfolio-based learning helps students build practical experience for opportunities in film production, advertising, OTT, digital media, and content creation.

FAQ's

1. Which Film Making courses are offered? ⌄

The current programs include the Master Program in Film Making plus Advanced Programs in Film Direction, Cinematography, Film Editing, and Digital Film Production.

2. What areas of filmmaking can I specialize in? ⌄

The course options allow focus on direction, cinematography, editing, and digital film production, along with the broader Master Program in Film Making.

3. Are AI-assisted workflows included in the film courses? ⌄

Yes. The listed programs use AI-integrated learning alongside filmmaking and digital production skills.

4. How long are the Film Making programs? ⌄

The Master Program is listed for 30 months, while the Advanced Programs are listed for 12 months each.

5. What is the eligibility for the Film Making courses? ⌄

The course listings state eligibility as any 10th pass or equivalent.

6. Will I learn film direction? ⌄

Yes. Film Direction is offered as a dedicated Advanced Program and is also part of the broader filmmaking learning pathway.

7. Is cinematography covered in the courses? ⌄

Yes. Cinematography is available as a dedicated Advanced Program.

8. Is film editing part of the curriculum? ⌄

Yes. Film Editing is offered as a dedicated Advanced Program and forms an important part of post-production workflow.

9. Do the courses include digital film production? ⌄

Yes. Digital Film Production is available as a dedicated Advanced Program within the Film Making course group.

10. Will practical projects help me build a portfolio? ⌄

Yes. The programs use practical, project-based learning to help students develop production experience and portfolio-ready work.
