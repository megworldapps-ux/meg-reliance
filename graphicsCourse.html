The Graphics & Digital Design courses at Reliance Animation Academy are built for learners who want to turn ideas into strong visual communication and digital experiences.

Students develop skills across graphic design, digital composition, visual storytelling, typography, layout, image creation, branding concepts, and creative production using industry-relevant workflows.

Hands-on assignments and portfolio projects help students improve design thinking, visual consistency, and presentation quality while exploring modern digital tools and AI-assisted creative workflows.

The programs support careers in graphic design, digital media, branding, content creation, creative studios, and related visual communication roles.

FAQ's

1. Which Graphics & Digital Design courses are available? ⌄

The current programs include the Master Program in Graphic & Digital Design, Advanced Program in Graphic Design, and Advanced Program in Digital Design.

2. What skills are covered in Graphics & Digital Design? ⌄

The program content focuses on graphic and digital design skills, practical creative production, visual communication, and portfolio-oriented work.

3. Are AI-assisted tools included? ⌄

Yes. The programs introduce AI-assisted workflows along with modern creative software to support design development and production.

4. How long are the listed programs? ⌄

The Master Program is listed for 30 months, while the Advanced Graphic Design and Advanced Digital Design programs are listed for 12 months each.

5. What is the eligibility for these design programs? ⌄

The course listings state eligibility as any 10th pass or equivalent.

6. Will I learn practical design workflows? ⌄

Yes. The learning approach combines guided creative practice with projects so students can apply design concepts to production work.

7. Will I build a design portfolio? ⌄

Yes. Portfolio development is part of the practical learning approach described for the programs.

8. Can the courses help with both graphic and digital design? ⌄

Yes. The course set includes separate paths for Graphic Design and Digital Design, as well as a broader Master Program in Graphic & Digital Design.

9. Who can benefit from these courses? ⌄

They are suited to learners who want structured training in creative design, digital production, and portfolio development.

10. What career directions can these programs support? ⌄

The programs are intended to build practical design skills for creative and digital media work, supported by project and portfolio development.
